02

项目概述与设计概念

LEGO House translates the logic of LEGO bricks into stacked public volumes, playable roof terraces, and color-coded interior learning experiences.

资料中的概念

BIG states that the project applies the ratio of the LEGO brick throughout the architecture and embodies LEGO culture and values.

综合判断

The project turns the LEGO system into an architectural rule: stacked volumes, accessible roof surfaces, color-coded experiences, and a ground-level public interface.

03

核心设计策略

01

Translate brand logic into spatial rules

面对的问题
A brand building can become a superficial icon if the symbol remains only on the facade.
具体做法
Use LEGO brick ratio, stacking, color, and combinatory logic to organize massing, facade order, roof terraces, and interior zones.
建筑效果
The brand is experienced through space and movement rather than only through signage or imagery.
证据
BIG describes the use of LEGO brick ratio; ArchDaily photos and drawings show stacked white volumes and color-coded roof and interior spaces.
可迁移方法
When a project has a strong cultural or brand motif, translate it into proportion, organization, circulation, or tectonic rules.

02

Turn the roof into a playable public terrain

面对的问题
Experience buildings often become inward-facing, ticketed destinations with weak urban publicness.
具体做法
Stack volumes to create accessible roof terraces and outdoor play surfaces.
建筑效果
The building becomes something to climb, inhabit, and use, not only something to enter and view.
证据
ArchDaily photos and section drawings show roof terraces as part of the architectural experience.
可迁移方法
Cultural and commercial public buildings can use roof, steps, terraces, and platforms as public interfaces.
图像 img2图像 img6 来源 s2
03

Organize learning through color-coded experiential zones

面对的问题
A family learning space needs active participation rather than passive exhibition viewing.
具体做法
Arrange different experience areas through themed zones, color, and paths inside the stacked building.
建筑效果
The visitor route becomes a sequence of making, playing, pausing, and discovering.
证据
LEGO House official materials emphasize play and experience; ArchDaily interior photos and plans show activity-based spaces.
可迁移方法
Educational and cultural projects should translate learning goals into spatial types, not only exhibition content.
图像 img3图像 img5 来源 s2来源 s3
04

Use section to connect interior experience and exterior public space

面对的问题
Exterior form, interior exhibitions, and roof activities can become separate gestures.
具体做法
Connect stacked volumes, floor heights, interior zones, and roof terraces through a continuous sectional organization.
建筑效果
The building works as a continuous public terrain from ground to roof, rather than a closed exhibition box.
证据
ArchDaily section drawing shows the relationship between stacked volumes, interior spaces, and roof platforms.
可迁移方法
Use sections to test whether arrival, movement, interior activities, and roof use form one coherent experience.
