信息缺口与冲突

  • Technical metrics：Height, FAR, floor count, and structural system are useful learning references but come mainly from lecture/reporting sources rather than formal planning or construction documents.
  • Client / owner wording：Sources use related but not fully identical client/owner descriptions, including Sichuan Mailun Industrial Co., Ltd. and Chengdu Beisen Investment Group. Normalize before publication.
  • Construction details：Material and tectonic logic is relatively well covered, but complete joint drawings, waterproofing layers, construction organization, and quality-control records were not found.
  • Image rights：Image links are retained for research reference only; public website, publication, or commercial display requires separate rights clearance.
